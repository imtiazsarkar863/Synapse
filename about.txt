SYNAPSE
=======

Synapse is an interactive DNA-to-protein learning simulator. It helps students
follow the flow of genetic information from DNA to RNA and finally to a chain
of amino acids.

WHAT THE SIMULATOR DOES
-----------------------

The simulator accepts a DNA sequence containing the bases A, T, G, and C.
Spaces, commas, and lowercase letters are handled automatically. After the
sequence is submitted, Synapse processes the result in three connected stages:

1. Replication
	The simulator creates the complementary DNA strand using base-pair rules:
	A pairs with T, and G pairs with C.

2. Transcription
	The DNA sequence is used to produce an mRNA sequence. During this step,
	thymine is represented by uracil in RNA, following the rules A to U, T to A,
	G to C, and C to G.

3. Translation
	The mRNA is divided into codons of three bases. Synapse searches for the
	start codon AUG, converts codons into amino acids, and stops translation
	when it reaches UAA, UAG, or UGA.

The simulator presents these stages asynchronously. Replication is completed
before transcription begins, and translation begins only after the mRNA result
is available. A progress indicator makes this sequence visible to the user.

LEARNING MATERIAL
-----------------

The page includes educational videos for replication, transcription, and
translation. Each video is muted and begins playing when it enters the visible
area of the page. The videos pause when they are scrolled away. Explanations
beneath the videos describe the biological role of each process.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------

Synapse uses a glass-style interface with translucent panels, blurred layers,
responsive layouts, and a fixed navigation bar. The page adapts to desktop and
mobile screens. The navigation links provide direct access to the simulator,
replication, transcription, and translation sections.

ACCOUNT FEATURES
----------------

The project includes a login interface with email-based demo authentication
and Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication. Google sign-in requires the
Firebase project configuration, an enabled Google provider, and the deployed
domain to be listed under Firebase Authorized Domains.

PROJECT STATUS
--------------

Synapse is an educational project and a work in progress. It is intended for
learning and visualization rather than medical, diagnostic, or research use.
The simulator demonstrates the basic central dogma of molecular biology and
does not replace a laboratory tool, textbook, or qualified scientific guidance.

The project will continue to receive improvements in biological coverage,
visual explanations, saved progress, accessibility, and interactive learning
features.
